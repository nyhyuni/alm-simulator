Model assumptions & limitations

Assets are a fixed weighted bond portfolio. Liabilities are level annual payments, without mortality, lapses, expenses, guarantees, or rebalancing. Rates are synthetic, with 252 steps per year; they are not calibrated to market data. Cash flows use continuous discounting along each path. PV01 shifts the entire rate path and holds cash flows fixed. Monetary values are model units, not an actual insurer's balance sheet. A negative surplus is not a regulatory solvency assessment.
